## Notes
Limiter for slower gets: the in-block linear walk. Records walked per get (10k index-key probes, raw):
  short ids: v6 42.8 records at 30.3 ns/record, v7 124.9 records at 14.4 ns/record (blocks 1908 -> 1049)
  uuid ids:  v6 25.3 records, v7 42.1 records (blocks 3025 -> 2156)
A 4 KiB block holds about 3x more short-id index records in v7. The walker was tightened from
~16 to ~9 ns/record (profiled with --cpu-prof), which does not offset 3x the records.
Tried a 2 KiB v7 block target (not committed): short-id raw gets 16.9 ms, lz4 gets 24.3 ms, back at
or under v6, with index-only raw bytes unchanged. But uuid all-lz4 grew to 6969172 bytes, over the
v6 6818889, so it fails the lz4 bytes bar.
Alternating A/B (3 invocations x 7 rounds per side) agreed with these medians: scans and decode within
range, gets raw short 14.9-17.7 -> 21.4-21.9 ms, gets lz4 uuid 34.4-35.5 -> 37.8-41.9 ms.
