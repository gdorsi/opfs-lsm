## Notes
Index-only raw bytes, short ids: 1870050 / 5017846 = 37.3% of v6. UUID ids: 61.3%.
LZ4 runs vs v6: short all 2124611 < 2377044, uuid all 6409092 < 6818889, uuid index-only 4508488 < 4879044.
Gets: a v7 block binary-searches its offset table (one u16 per 4th record) and walks at most 4 records,
instead of walking ~125 records (short ids) per get. Scans: the run cursor walks a block in place and
copies each record once, instead of decoding the whole block into entries; key copy is one slice plus
the prefix written over its head.
Limiter after the change, from --cpu-prof of isolated gets: LZ4 gets are bound by the wasm block
decompress and its copies across the wasm boundary (~45% of samples). v7 UUID blocks compress less
(3.8 KB vs 2.5 KB per packed block), so each get decompresses more input. Raw gets split between the
bloom/range check and the in-block binary search.
In-bench get timings are bimodal on v7 (short raw 9.3 or 12.5 ms per invocation, the mode follows which
op absorbs GC); isolated ops above remove that context. Both are under v6.
